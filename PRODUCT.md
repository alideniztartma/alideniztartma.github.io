# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People discovering Ali Deniz Tartma through GitHub, open-source communities, developer networks, or a shared project. They want a quick, credible sense of who he is, what he contributes to, and where to explore his work.

## Product Purpose

alideniz.me is Ali Deniz Tartma's personal biography and open-source portfolio. It should make his identity, interests, contributions, projects, and contact paths understandable within one visit.

## Positioning

The site presents a young developer's public learning and contribution trail as a living open-source map: practical contributions and independent projects are the proof, not invented metrics or testimonials.

## Operating Context

This is a static GitHub Pages site with no build system. Visitors may arrive from desktop or mobile, often from an external profile or repository link, and should be able to open GitHub destinations directly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the confirmed biography, contribution descriptions, project names, and outbound links in the existing page.
- Preserve Turkish and English language switching, automatic language detection, and light/dark theme preference.
- Keep the implementation dependency-free and runnable as a single static page.
- Do not invent clients, testimonials, metrics, dates, awards, or capabilities.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ali Deniz Tartma
- Website: https://alideniz.me
- Voice: curious, direct, technically grounded, and open-source friendly.
- Confirmed links: GitHub, Mastodon, email, NASA F Prime, MDN, React, Awesome Python, GitCanvas, and Vessel Journal.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing `index.html` contains the real contribution records, project descriptions, social links, bilingual copy, and theme/language behavior. No additional imagery or analytics claims are confirmed.

## Product Principles

- Lead with real work rather than generic self-description.
- Make exploration effortless from the first viewport.
- Keep technical curiosity human and legible.
- Use motion to create orientation and continuity, not distraction.
- Never trade factual clarity for visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, adequate contrast, descriptive link and image labels, touch-friendly controls, and a reduced-motion mode.
